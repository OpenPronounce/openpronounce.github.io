Reading a result

A result is a JSON document. Check reliable before using the scores, and the scores before the words.

A real result, shortened

{
  "score": 36.2,
  "scores": { "total": 36.2, "accuracy": 36.6, "fluency": 55.0, "prosodic": 45.9 },
  "reliable": false,
  "reasons": ["not said: good"],
  "differences": {
    "errors": [
      { "word": "good", "position": 4, "expected": "ɡʊd", "actual": "dɪ", "confidence": 0.879, "missing": true, "phones": [...] },
      { "word": "designer", "position": 5, "expected": "dɪzaɪnɚ", "actual": "vɛləpɚ", "confidence": 0.886,
        "phones": [ { "expected": "d", "heard": "v", "confidence": 0.643, "start": 1.02, "end": 1.04 }, ... ] }
    ],
    "alignment": { "sentence_probability": 0.078, "reliable": false, ... }
  },
  "audio_quality": { "duration": 2.926, "level_db": -43.1, "snr_db": 31.3, "issues": [] }
}

Read speech

FieldMeaning
score0 to 100, the total of scores
scorestotal, accuracy, fluency, prosodic, ten times the raters' 0 to 10 scale
reliable, reasonsFalse when the recording does not match the sentence, when words were not said, when much was said in excess or when the audio is unfit. The scores are still there; do not show them as they are.
transcribeWhat the word recognizer heard
differences.errors[]One entry per word that went wrong: word, position, expected and actual sounds in IPA, confidence (probability the raters would reject it), phones[] with each sound's expected, heard, confidence, start, end, and missing when the word was not said
differences.words[]The same for every word, flagged or not, with its time span and mispronounced
differences.extra[]Speech that is not in the sentence: after, before (positions around it), start, end, heard
differences.alignmentWhether the recording is a reading of the sentence at all: sentence_probability, reliable
differences.heard_phonesEvery sound recognised in the audio
audio_qualityduration, level_db, clipping_ratio, snr_db and issues: too_short, silent, too_quiet, clipped, noisy
prosodyPitch and loudness curves, ready to draw
formula_score, acoustic_distanceThe formula of versions before 0.4 and its distance to a synthetic native reading, kept for comparison

Free speech

FieldMeaning
mode"free"
score, scoresThe same four dimensions, averaged over the segments by duration
transcribeThe transcript everything is measured against
words[]Every word with start, end (seconds in the whole recording), confidence that it was unclear, unclear, expected and heard
unclear_wordsThe words above the threshold
segments[]The parts cut at the pauses, each with start, end, transcribe, scores
duration, speech_durationSeconds
audio_quality, reliable, reasonsAs for read speech; reliable is false when there is too little speech

Showing it to your users

Turn the total into a band rather than a decimal: two scores a few points apart are the same performance. Bands that read well: 90 and above excellent, 70 to 89 good, 50 to 69 understandable with clear errors, under 50 hard to follow. Then list at most three flagged words, with a button that replays each one from its start to its end.
