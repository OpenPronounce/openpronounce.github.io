Star Install

Read a sentence aloud. See what a rater would mark.

Pick a sentence, record yourself reading it, and the page scores it with the same pipeline as the Python library. It all runs on this device, so the recording never leaves it.

Before you press Record

The first visit downloads about 130 MB. Most of it is the speech model, and your browser keeps it, so later visits do not download it again. On a metered connection, wait for Wi-Fi.

Nothing is uploaded. The recording is analysed by this page, on your device. No server receives it, and you can check that in your browser’s network tab.

English, read aloud, only. The page needs the sentence you meant to read. Free speech and the other languages are in the Python library.

The score can be a couple of points away from the Python library’s. To fit in a browser, the page runs a smaller model distilled from the library’s, with its weights rounded to 8-bit integers. Given that same small model, the library and the page flag the same words on nearly every recording, and their totals differ by less than a point on average.
