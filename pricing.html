The same software, whichever licence you hold

There is no community edition and no premium edition. A company paying 12,000 € a year runs exactly what anyone can install with pip install openpronounce, and can read every line of it.

The figures on this site come with the scripts that reproduce them, and the models can be retrained on your own ratings. If maintenance stopped tomorrow, nothing you depend on would disappear.

The licences pay for what open source rarely funds: maintenance, answers to questions, and the work on the next languages.

Licensing questions

What does the AGPL require?

If you distribute a product built on OpenPronounce, or let people use it over a network, you must publish that product's source code under the AGPL. Using it internally to test or measure it requires nothing.

What counts as one product?

What you sell or offer under one name: an application, a platform, an API. The web, iOS and Android versions of the same application are one product. Two applications sold under different names are two.

Is there a limit on recordings, users or servers?

No.

What if our revenue changes during the year?

The price is set at renewal, on the fiscal year that has just closed.

What does the licence not cover?

Three separate components keep their own licence: espeak-ng (GPL-3), which the languages other than English need and which runs as a separate program; the optional Piper voices; and the mouth drawings of the web demo. English needs none of them. See Licensing.

We are a non-profit or a public body.

Research is covered by the AGPL. For a closed service, get in touch: the price is based on your annual budget rather than a revenue.
