Thank you for your order

The licence for your product and the invoice will reach your billing email within one working day. You can ship from today: the licence year starts on the date of payment.

  1. 1

    Keep the licence with your product's records

    It names your company and your product. There is no key to install and no server to contact; OpenPronounce runs the same way with or without it.

  2. 2

    Measure it on your own recordings, if not done yet

    A CSV of rated recordings and openpronounce-evaluate. Send the report and you will get help reading it. How to measure.

  3. 3

    Follow the releases

    Watch the repository on GitHub to be told about new versions and languages. Every release published during your licence year is included.

  4. 4

    Ask when you need to

    Integration, audio quality, how to present scores: use the contact form.
